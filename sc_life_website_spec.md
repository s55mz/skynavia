
# SC LIFE Website Renewal Specification (Wireframe & Features)

## Overview
This document outlines the proposed structure, wireframe, and functional requirements for the renewal of the SC LIFE corporate website.
The primary goal is to create a **B2B electronics sourcing website that increases inquiries and quote requests**.

Key goals:
- Increase **quote requests**
- Improve **trust and credibility**
- Enable **part number search**
- Clarify **services and procurement capabilities**
